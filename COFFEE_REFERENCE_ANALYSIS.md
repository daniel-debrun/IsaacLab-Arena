The visible ordering is meaningful. Raising the dispenser precedes extracting the tray; extracting the tray exposes the removable grounds container. The bin is made accessible and uncovered before the container is picked up for transport. The grounds container is reseated in the tray before the entire assembly is reinserted. The food-waste lid is restored before the drawer is closed.

The final demonstrated state is: bulk grounds deposited into the food-waste bin, residual grounds allowed in the container, grounds container seated in its tray, complete tray installed, food-waste lid seated, drawer closed, and hand released. The raised dispenser is an intentional final-state difference from the opening frames. The temporary tray and lid support surfaces are clear again.
